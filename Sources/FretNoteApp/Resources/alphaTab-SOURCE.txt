build.mjs records the GPIF parser changes (zero-valued bend midpoints and
unused midpoint fields beyond the destination). It generates a patched copy
from the pinned upstream npm package. importer.js preserves bend control points
and composes bend/vibrato MIDI events. The resulting parser is bundled in this app.
